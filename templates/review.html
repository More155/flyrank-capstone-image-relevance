Get a suggestion asks the real system "what image best fits this post?" A good match comes back with a thumbnail, a similarity score, and Approve/Reject buttons. If nothing qualifies, it comes back as no_match with no image on purpose — the guard refuses to show a wrong picture rather than guess, so that row is supposed to look empty.

Force a specific image is the demo trick: pick "Getting to Know the Red Fox" as the post and any gray_wolf image, then watch it get refused even though you forced it — the guard can't be tricked into endorsing a bad pairing.
